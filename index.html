About Kupa Warner

Brisbane Videographer & Cinematographer with 15+ Years Industry Experience

I'm Kupa Warner, a Brisbane-based cinematographer and videographer specialising in commercial, corporate, event and music video shoots.

With 15+ years of professional experience, I've built a career crafting visually compelling stories for global brands, major events, and ambitious creative projects. My work spans commercial campaigns, social media content, music videos, live events, documentary storytelling, and government productions.

My cinematography has been recognized with a Best Cinematography award at the Vaughan International Film Festival. I've served as cinematographer for Conor McGregor branded content and as Lead Videographer for major Australian events including PAX Australia and SXSW Australia.

From leading 7-person camera and lighting crews to solo shoots, I bring technical expertise in Sony FX6 cinema cameras, advanced lighting and aerial cinematography with the DJI Mavic 3.

Based in Brisbane, Queensland, I work with clients across Brisbane CBD, Fortitude Valley, South Brisbane, West End, New Farm, Gold Coast, and Sunshine Coast - shooting cinematic footage that elevates brands and tells stories that resonate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Brisbane Video Production FAQ

What areas of Brisbane do you service?

I provide videography services across all Brisbane areas including Brisbane CBD, Fortitude Valley, South Brisbane, West End, New Farm, Paddington, Woolloongabba, and surrounding suburbs. I also regularly work on the Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast for commercial and event video production projects.

What types of video production do you specialize in?

I work as a cinematographer and videographer on commercial, corporate, event, music video and social media shoots. My experience spans TVCs, brand films, training videos, conference coverage, documentary-style productions, and platform-optimized content for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

What equipment do you use for video production?

I shoot on professional cinema-grade equipment including the Sony FX6 cinema camera, Sony A7III, DJI Mavic 3 for aerial cinematography, professional lighting setups, gimbals, Steadicam systems, and multi-camera broadcast configurations.

How much does a videographer cost in Brisbane?

Rates depend on the shoot: how many days, the crew and the equipment involved. Contact me for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs. I offer competitive rates for Brisbane businesses while maintaining cinema-grade image quality.

Do you work with large production crews?

Yes, I regularly lead multi-person production crews and can scale teams based on project requirements. From solo shoots for quick-turnaround content to full-scale productions requiring multiple camera operators, lighting technicians, and sound engineers, I adapt to your production needs.

How long does a shoot take?

It depends on the shoot. Most social and event shoots are a half or full day. Commercial shoots typically run one to several days, depending on locations and setups. Footage is handed over promptly after the shoot, ready for your editor.

What do you deliver?

I focus on cinematography and camera work. I deliver high-quality, well-organised footage ready for your editor or post-production team, and I work alongside your producer or agency to bring the creative vision to life on set.
